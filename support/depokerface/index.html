DePokerface Support

A free, offline imposter party game for 3 to 10 players.

How to play

1. Add names and deal. Pass the phone; each player holds a thumb down to peek at the secret word. The imposter doesn't get it.

2. One by one, everyone says one word that fits the secret. The imposter has to bluff.

3. On three, everyone points at who they suspect.

4. The Detective (the front camera) accuses someone too, from their face during the peek, and plays the slow-motion evidence. Beat it.

New to the game? Leave "New players? Teach as we go" on in setup and the first round explains each step.

Common questions

Do I need the internet or an account? No. Everything runs on the phone.

Can we play without the camera? Yes. Choose "Play without it", or turn the camera off in Settings. The Detective then works from timing alone.

Own phones won't connect. Put the phones on the same Wi-Fi, or keep them close with Bluetooth on, and make sure every phone has the latest version. On iPhone, allow Local Network for DePokerface in Settings > Privacy & Security.

How do I delete saved clips? Settings > Delete every clip removes the copies DePokerface keeps. Clips you saved to Photos are removed in the Photos app.

Is the Detective a lie detector? No. It spots who reacted most differently from the group. It's a game, and it's often wrong on purpose.
