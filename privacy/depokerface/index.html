Privacy Policy

DePokerface · Last updated: November 14, 2026

DePokerface has no user accounts, runs no servers, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics or tracking. The camera is read live on your phone and the frames are thrown away. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

No accounts, no sign-up

DePokerface doesn't ask for your name, email or any other identifying information. Player names you type are used only to run the game on your phone.

The camera

If you allow it, the front camera runs during each secret peek so the in-game Detective can read expression signals: smile, brow raise, lip press, eye movement, blinks and head movement. This is done on your phone by Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker model, which ships inside the app and works offline. DePokerface does not build a face template, does not identify anyone, and keeps nothing after the round. A red dot shows whenever the camera is on. You can play without the camera at any time; the Detective then works from timing alone. The Detective is entertainment only: it is guessing, not detecting lies.

The replay buffer

To show the Detective's slow-motion replay, the last couple of seconds of each peek are held in the phone's memory during the round. They are cleared at the start of every round, when the night ends, when the app goes to the background and when it starts, and are never written to storage unless you tap Save or Share.

Replay clips

A video clip is created only when you tap Save or Share, after the app asks whether everyone in the clip is OK with posting. Save adds it to your Photos (DePokerface asks only for permission to add, not to read your library), and the app then deletes its own file. Share hands it to the app you choose and the app deletes its file when the share sheet closes; what happens next is governed by the app you chose. DePokerface keeps no clips.

Own phones mode

When players use their own phones, the phones connect directly to each other on your local Wi-Fi or nearby over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. They exchange only player names, roles, word identifiers, votes, timers and each player's tell score. No video, images or face data ever leave a phone, and nothing passes through a server. Joining uses the camera to scan the host's QR code; the scan is read on the phone and not kept.

Voice, sound and the pack maker

The Detective's voice uses your phone's built-in speech, on the phone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, "Make a pack" drafts word lists with Apple's on-device model; your theme stays on the phone.

Age signals

Turning on the adults-only Spicy pack asks the App Store's age-range signal (where available) or asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. DePokerface uses the answer only to switch the pack on or keep it off, and stores only that on/off setting.

What is stored on your phone

Your last group of names, the pack and mode you picked, settings, your own word lists and packs, and simple counts such as rounds played and how often the Detective was right. All of this stays in the app's storage on your phone. Like most apps it may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which are governed by your device and Apple. Deleting the app deletes all of it (clips you saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there).

No advertising, tracking or analytics

DePokerface includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries. We receive no data about you or how you use the app. Downloads are counted only by Apple's own App Store statistics.

Children

DePokerface does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
