Eaven privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Eaven is a free remote control app for smart TVs. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Eaven has no accounts and no servers. Your TV list stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves your phone is what the ad network needs to show ads.

What stays on your phone

  • The TVs you pair with: their names, network addresses and the pairing keys they give us. These are stored in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain). They are never sent anywhere.
  • Your settings and a few counters (for example how many times you opened the app). These stay on the phone and are never sent anywhere.
  • Eaven talks to your TV directly over your home Wi-Fi. It does not use a server of ours.

Photos and videos on your TV

If you use the optional photo and video feature, Eaven asks for access to the photos and videos on your phone so you can choose one item. Only that one item is shown on your TV: Eaven sends it directly to the TV over your home Wi-Fi, from the phone, using a temporary web address that only devices on your home network can open. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Widgets, shortcuts and Apple Watch

The home-screen widget, Control Center buttons, Siri shortcuts and the Apple Watch app only send the same button presses to your TV. They use the TV list already stored on your phone, and nothing is sent to us.

Ads

The free version of Eaven shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the ad network may collect and use data from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), app interactions and diagnostics. Eaven does not give the ad network your TV names, addresses or pairing keys.

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states) Eaven asks for your choice using Google's consent tool before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy choices when it is offered.
  • iPhone and iPad. Eaven asks for permission to track only after your first successful button press, with an explanation first. If you say no, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier.
  • Reset your advertising ID. On Android: Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads (the path varies by phone). On iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • No ads with Eaven Plus. If you buy Eaven Plus, the app stops requesting ads.

Purchases

Eaven Plus is bought through Google Play or the App Store. They process the payment; Eaven never sees your payment details. Eaven checks on your phone, using the store's purchase information, whether Plus is active.

Children

Eaven is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Permissions

  • Local network / Wi-Fi. To find and control TVs on your home network.
  • Photos. Only when you choose a photo or video to show on your TV (iPhone and iPad).
  • Internet. Used by the ad network and the store.
  • Advertising ID. Used by the ad network (Android 13 and later).
